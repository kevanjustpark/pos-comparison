Clarifications and Notes

Transaction Fees: Listed fees (e.g., 1.5–2.9% + $0.10–$0.30) are blended rates, inclusive of interchange, payment processing, and assessment fees, unless noted (e.g., Global Payments, Shift4 may offer interchange-plus for stadiums, separating interchange from markup). Additional fees (e.g., gateway, chargeback, PCI compliance) are separate.

Hardware Costs:

  • Selling: Costs (e.g., $49 for Square readers, $500–$2,000 for Cantaloupe kiosks) are for outright purchase.
  • Leasing: Long-term (12–36 months) for stadiums; payments often bundled with software (e.g., Shift4's free terminals with contract).
  • Renting: Short-term (days to months) for events; per-device or per-event costs (e.g., atVenu's $50–$200/device for 1–7 days).
  • Renting requires sophisticated logistics (e.g., shipping, returns, maintenance); leasing/selling involves simpler one-time shipments.

Software Subscription:

  • Most providers (e.g., Square, Cantaloupe, MyVenue) charge per venue, covering all terminals, ideal for stadiums with many devices.
  • Some (e.g., Global Payments, Volanté) use a hybrid model, with per-venue base fees and per-terminal add-ons ($10–$50/month) for extra devices or features.
  • Event-based fees (e.g., atVenu's $500–$2,000/event) cover all terminals for temporary setups.

Data Gaps: Exact terms for Shift4, Global Payments, Cantaloupe, MyVenue, TCPOS, Volanté, and accesso ShoWare are custom. Estimates are based on competitors (e.g., Square, SpotOn) and industry norms.
