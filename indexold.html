Notes

Hardware Costs: Many companies (e.g., Shift4, SpotOn) offer free or low-cost hardware with long-term contracts to reduce upfront costs, especially for high-volume venues like stadiums.

Software Subscription: Pricing varies widely based on features (e.g., analytics, mobile ordering, ticketing integrations). Enterprise-grade solutions (e.g., Shift4, MyVenue) often require custom quotes.

Transaction Fees: Stadiums process high transaction volumes, so providers offer competitive rates (1.5–2.5%) with negotiable terms for large clients.

Value-Added Services: Analytics, integrations, and premium support are high-margin upsells critical for venues prioritizing fan experience.

Data Gaps: Companies like Global Payments, Cantaloupe, MyVenue, TCPOS, Volanté, Seatlab, and accesso ShoWare do not publicly disclose exact pricing, so estimates are based on industry norms and competitor data.
